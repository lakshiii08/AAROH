AAROH - Multilingual Visual Flashcards

💧
येर (Yer)
English: Water | Hindi: पानी (Paani)
📖 Water is life for all living beings.
🏡 Gaon Example: Well and handpump water in village.
🏺
गदगा (Gadga)
English: Pot | Hindi: मटका (Matka)
📖 Traditional clay vessel that cools water naturally.
🏡 Gaon Example: Mitti ka matka kept under neem tree.
🌧️
पिर (Pir)
English: Rain | Hindi: बारिश (Baarish)
📖 Water falling from clouds that fills village ponds.
🏡 Gaon Example: First monsoon shower turning fields green.
🧱
बोडी (Bodi)
English: Well | Hindi: कुआँ (Kuan)
📖 Deep ground water source for village families.
🏡 Gaon Example: Ramu drawing water with bucket from well.
